## Full description
Arc & Ledger Tax Tools puts a boutique Enrolled Agent firm's free calculators and intake tools directly inside your AI assistant.

Ask about an IRS letter and get what it means, your real deadline, and what to do. Ask about foreign accounts and learn whether you must file the FBAR or Form 8938, with the exact thresholds and catch-up options. Wondering if an S-Corp would save you money, or how much estimated tax to pay this quarter? The tools run the numbers from published rates and show the break-even, not a sales pitch.

Every answer is general information, not tax advice, and ends with an optional handoff to an Enrolled Agent - the person who can actually represent you before the IRS. The firm is led by an Enrolled Agent enrolled to practice before the IRS since 2016, publishes flat fees, and works in English, Turkish, and Spanish.

No account, no login, nothing stored. The tools answer first; a human is there only when you want one.
