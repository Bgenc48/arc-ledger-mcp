# Federal tax-balance resolution orientation

This is an orientation aid for the `resolve-back-taxes` Skill. It is not a
determination that any path is available or appropriate.

Official starting points:

- Payments and payment plans: https://www.irs.gov/payments
- Offer in Compromise: https://www.irs.gov/payments/offer-in-compromise
- Penalties: https://www.irs.gov/payments/penalties
- Collection process: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p594.pdf

## Typical sequence

1. Identify any active notice, levy, hearing, or petition deadline.
2. Verify which required returns are filed.
3. Verify assessed balances and current payoff figures from the IRS account or
   transcripts.
4. Review current collection alternatives against the user's complete
   financial facts.
5. Keep new filings and required payments current while a request is pending.

The IRS determines the required compliance period and eligibility from the
account and facts. Do not turn a general rule of thumb into a fixed result.

## Common paths

| Path | General screening idea | Common form or channel |
|---|---|---|
| Short-term payment | The verified balance can be paid within the current IRS short-term period | IRS online account or phone |
| Installment agreement | Monthly payment is feasible and filing compliance is current | Online application or Form 9465 |
| Financial-disclosure agreement | The balance or proposed payment requires a collection financial statement | Form 433-F, 433-A, or 433-B as applicable |
| Offer in Compromise | A full facts-and-circumstances screen may show a recognized basis for compromise | Form 656 and the applicable OIC financial statement |
| Currently Not Collectible | Paying would prevent necessary living expenses | Collection financial statement and supporting records |
| Penalty relief | First-time criteria or documented reasonable cause may apply | IRS request channel or Form 843 where applicable |

These are categories, not promises. Thresholds, fees, forms, and procedures
change. Use the official source returned by the connector.

## Special caution

Business payroll taxes, trust-fund recovery exposure, active levies,
bankruptcy, jointly assessed liabilities, innocent-spouse issues, and expiring
statutory periods need individualized review. Do not extrapolate a consumer
payment-plan screen to those situations.

Do not put transcripts, returns, account numbers, or supporting documents into
the connector.
