# IRS notice map (collection sequence)

Use this to explain how a notice fits the sequence when the tools are
unavailable. Deadlines run from the notice date printed on the letter, not the
day it arrived. These are general references, not a substitute for reading the
specific notice.

## The balance-due / collection ladder

1. **CP14** - first bill. A balance the IRS says is due on a filed return.
   Typically 21 days. Often triggered by a misapplied payment.
2. **CP501** - second reminder of the same balance. Typically 21 days.
3. **CP503** - urgent reminder. Often 21 days, sometimes 10.
4. **CP504** - Notice of Intent to Levy. Can seize your state tax refund. 30
   days. NOT yet the final levy notice and does NOT grant a hearing right.
5. **LT11 / Letter 1058 / CP90** - Final Notice of Intent to Levy. 30 days to
   request a Collection Due Process (CDP) hearing on Form 12153. A timely
   request generally pauses bank and wage levies and preserves Tax Court review.

## Underreporter / deficiency track

- **CP2000** - proposed change from the Automated Underreporter system after a
  third-party form did not match the return. 30 days (60 if outside the US). Not
  an audit; the proposed amount is often wrong (missing cost basis, gross 1099-K
  totals). Respond on the CP2000 response form, not a 1040-X.
- **CP3219A** - Statutory Notice of Deficiency, the "90-day letter." 90 days
  (150 if outside the US) to petition the U.S. Tax Court. This deadline CANNOT
  be extended by anyone.

## Resolution paths (once filing-compliant)

- **Short-term payment plan** - pay in full within about 180 days.
- **Installment agreement** - streamlined at $50,000 or less (no financial
  disclosure); above that, Form 433-F/433-A financials, possibly partial-pay.
- **Offer in Compromise** - settle for less based on doubt as to collectibility.
  A fit-check only; the IRS accepts a minority of offers. Form 656 + 433-A(OIC).
- **Currently Not Collectible** - hardship pause when paying would prevent basic
  living expenses. Form 433-F. Penalties and interest keep accruing.
- **Penalty abatement** - first-time (clean prior 3-year history) or
  reasonable-cause. Form 843 or by phone. Interest is removed only if the
  underlying penalty is.

## Representation forms

- **Form 8821** (Tax Information Authorization) - lets an Enrolled Agent pull
  your IRS transcripts to see the real balance and dates.
- **Form 2848** (Power of Attorney) - lets an Enrolled Agent speak to the IRS
  and negotiate on your behalf.

## Collection statute

The IRS generally has 10 years from assessment to collect (the Collection
Statute Expiration Date, IRC 6502). Some resolution steps pause or extend it.
